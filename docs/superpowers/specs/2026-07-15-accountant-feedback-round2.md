Verbatim transcription of "Features for Software.docx" from SparkingAsia's accountant.

**Features for Software**

**File**
1. Open or restore a company.
2. Create a backup company — Restore a backup company.
3. Backup copy — for loss of company backup or file.
4. Multi user Mode — Admin & Accountant.
5. Print reports / forms.

**Customers**
- Customer Center — list of all customers — add or delete customers.
- Estimates — for customers, estimated cost of any project or services.
- Sales Order — records goods or services a customer wants to buy.
- Sales Invoice — request for payment sent to a customer before you receive their money.

**Vendor / Suppliers**
- Vendor Center — add or delete vendors/suppliers.
- Enter Bills — record that a payment is due and we need to pay.

**Employees**
- Employees Center — add or delete employees with names, designations.

**Financials — Reports**
- Cash Flow Statement.
- Income & Expenses Graph.
- Employees & Payroll reports (specifically related to payroll / salary).
- Sales report.
- Sales graph.
- Accounts Payable graph — Vendor.
- Adjusted Trial Balance.
- Adjusted Journal / Ledger.
- Budgets and Forecast reports — Actual vs Budgeted.

**Charts of Accounts**
- Chart of Accounts.
- Sub Accounts of main accounts.
- Journal Entries — Entry Number.
- Classes — used to separate income & expenses based on their nature.
- Items.
- Sub items.
